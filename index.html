Case Study Audit

Current page: thistle.group/kitchens/case-studies/

Strong raw material.
Significant structural issues.

1
H1 has no keyword value. "Our Kitchen Projects" contains no style, location or service term. A more descriptive H1 would immediately strengthen relevance signals.
2
Meta title not yet customised. The page is currently using a site-wide template title. A bespoke meta title written specifically for this page is a straightforward, high-impact improvement.
3
Meta description not yet customised. As with the title, a bespoke description written for this page would significantly improve click-through rate from search results.
4
Creative titles are occupying the H2. "Greystone and Gloss", "Angle Delight" etc. are evocative and on-brand. Moving descriptive text to H2 level - with the creative names as stylistic subtitles - adds keyword value without losing the personality.
5
Projects currently show specifications rather than narrative. Adding written context around each project - the brief, the design decisions, the outcome - gives Google substantially more to index and gives visitors a stronger reason to enquire.
6
Projects currently share a single URL. Individual project pages would each be independently rankable for their own combination of style and location terms, creating meaningful additional search visibility.
7
Internal linking not yet in place. Once the hub structure is confirmed, linking each case study to its relevant style and location pages will create a strong link network across the site. This is covered in the URL architecture section.
8
Image alt text could be more descriptive. With galleries of 20-50 images per project, there is a significant opportunity to reinforce style and location keywords through more detailed alt text.
9
Schema markup not yet implemented. Adding Article or LocalBusiness structured data to individual project pages would improve eligibility for rich results in Google.
10
Finance is a powerful call to action waiting to be used. Visitors browsing completed projects are often asking "can I afford this?" A clear finance call to action on this page - highlighting available payment options - could have a material impact on enquiry rates.
The Strategic Question

Before restructuring

One question determines
the entire architecture

How many case studies can Thistle Group commit to writing - and how frequently will new ones be added?

This single answer determines whether the correct structure is a standalone hub, embedded content, or a hybrid of both. The right answer with the wrong architecture wastes content effort and may actively harm rankings.

Current position: 8 completed projects exist. That is enough to justify a hub - but only if the format is rebuilt correctly and the content commitment is there.

Note: the existing projects have excellent raw material - video, large photo galleries, and full specifications. The gap is written narrative, not creative assets.

Volume threshold guide

<6 Fewer than 6 projects

Embed into style pages. Do not build a hub yet - it will look sparse and thin.

6-12 The current position

Slim hub with individual pages. Also embed excerpts in style and location pages. This is where Thistle sits now.

12+ 12 or more projects

Full hub with category navigation - filter by style, location and kitchen type.

Option A
Option A

Embed case studies into
style and location pages

SEO & commercial benefits

Style and location pages gain additional unique content depth, strengthening topical authority.
Real project detail provides natural long-tail keyword variation without keyword stuffing.
Social proof appears at the right moment - a user on the Shaker Kitchens page who sees a nearby shaker project is far more likely to enquire.
No risk of a thin hub page. Easier to manage at lower content volumes.
Shorter click journey - proof matches product, user stays on the commercial page.

Drawbacks

Individual projects cannot rank independently. No dedicated landing page for long-tail queries like "traditional kitchen pitmedden aberdeenshire".
No portfolio section - nowhere to demonstrate breadth and range of completed work.
Harder to share individual projects directly with prospects in a sales context.
Same project on multiple pages requires distinct excerpts - not copy-pasted content - to avoid duplicate content concerns.

Use Option A when: fewer than 8 projects are available, or Thistle cannot commit to writing 400-600 words of narrative per project.

Option B
Option B

Standalone hub with
individual case study pages

Each project gets its own URL

Each page ranks independently for long-tail queries: "handleless kitchen kingswells aberdeen", "german kitchen aberdeen", "traditional kitchen aberdeenshire".
Individual pages accumulate their own authority and can earn inbound links over time.
The hub page ranks for "kitchen case studies Aberdeen", "kitchen projects aberdeenshire" - genuine navigational queries.
Each page supports Article and LocalBusiness schema - rich result eligibility.
A library of project pages is a powerful E-E-A-T signal - demonstrating genuine expertise and track record.

Portfolio effect: sales consultants can send a prospect directly to the most relevant project. Each page has its own CTA for A/B testing.

How to Write a Case Study

Writing guide

A case study is not a spec sheet.
It is a story with keywords.

Every case study must answer four questions:

1

The brief - what did the customer need?

2

The solution - what did we design and why?

3

The result - what was the outcome?

4

The CTA - how can I start my own project?

Title tag (70 chars): [Style] Kitchen Installation in [Location] | Thistle Group
H1: [Style] Kitchen Installation - [Area/Location]
Intro para: 100-150 words. Set the scene - location, style chosen, one-sentence outcome.

Recommended heading structure

Hero image - one strong shot directly below the H1 and intro paragraph.
H2: The Brief - client goals, constraints, challenges. 100-150 words.
H2: Design and Planning - range selection, layout decisions, bespoke elements. 150-200 words.
H3: [Range Name] - Why We Chose It - links naturally to the collection page and style page. 80-100 words.
H3: Full Specification - the existing spec list, now with narrative context above it.
H2: The Finished Kitchen - overview paragraph describing the space. 100-150 words.
Video embed - sits here, after the overview paragraph.
H3: [Sub-area] + gallery - one H3 per distinct element visible in the gallery (cabinets, island, utility room etc.). Built from available images - no fixed template.
Full project gallery - complete gallery after the sub-area sections.
H2: What Our Customer Said - genuine quote. Even one sentence transforms E-E-A-T and conversion.
H2: Start Your Own Project - CTA section. Mention finance. Link to the quote page.
